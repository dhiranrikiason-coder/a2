3. Safe Thermal Operation, Oven Use & Customer Maintenance

SupperPlatter stoneware pieces are formulated with refractory feldspathic minerals and vitrified at cone 10 temperatures exceeding 2,340°F, granting them exceptional thermal absorption properties when pre-warmed for evening dinner services. However, customers are contractually required to adhere strictly to our thermal operating protocols, which mandate gradual pre-heating in conventional ovens not exceeding 250°F for gentle plate-warming or 500°F for baking applications. Severe thermal shock resulting from moving a frozen or refrigerated platter directly into a pre-heated high-temperature oven, or placing a blistering 450°F stoneware vessel onto a cold stone or wet stainless steel surface, voids all structural breakage warranties. Customers must always utilize heat-resistant silicone trivets or woven wooden mats beneath hot serving platters to protect underlying dining room furniture and prevent localized thermal shock fracture.

Our solid timber carving boards, crafted from kiln-dried American black walnut, demand dedicated hand maintenance and must never be exposed to dishwasher cycles, standing soak water, or radiant heat lamps. Failure to re-condition timber boards every four to six weeks with food-grade mineral oil or organic beeswax accelerates moisture loss, resulting in potential grain lifting, warping, or joint stress fractures that are expressly excluded from warranty coverage. Detailed maintenance manuals and oiling schedules are included inside every packaging crate and remain accessible through our digital technical journal at all times. Customers assume full operational responsibility for following these care instructions, ensuring their dinnerware remains safe, sanitary, and structurally sound across decades of convivial family gatherings.

While we partner exclusively with premier international carriers who specialize in fragile luxury cargo, unexpected transit turbulence or rough handling may occasionally cause accidental transit damage. Every shipment is fully insured by SupperPlatter for its declared commercial value, providing our patrons with comprehensive financial protection throughout cross-country and international transit corridors.

Upon receipt of your shipment, the customer or authorized recipient must inspect the exterior shipping crate for signs of crushing, puncture, or dropping prior to signing the delivery receipt. In the unfortunate event that a ceramic platter arrives with structural cracks, edge chipping, or transit fractures, the customer must document the damage with high-resolution digital photographs of both the damaged vessel and the packaging crate within seventy-two hours of delivery.
