Privacy Policy & Data Stewardship

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Comprehensive Legal Review: September 2026

1. Core Pillar of Data Privacy & Architectural Commitment

At SupperPlatter Atelier LLC, we hold the privacy of our culinary patrons and dinner party hosts to the same exacting standards that guide our physical dinnerware craftsmanship. When you explore our digital collections, request customized table dimensions, or schedule an atelier appointment, our web infrastructure is engineered to process your personal identifiers with uncompromising discretion and cryptographic security. We strictly reject the commercial monetization of client telemetry and do not sell, license, or broker personal records to third-party marketing networks or analytical data aggregators. Every byte of operational data collected through our portal is utilized exclusively to deliver tailored dinnerware recommendations, process bespoke orders, and uphold statutory consumer warranty protections. Our administrative data retention schedules are routinely audited to purge obsolete client identifiers once transactional fulfillment and mandatory tax documentation intervals have lapsed.

This comprehensive Privacy Policy delineates the exact categories of personal identifiers we collect, the precise legal grounds under which processing occurs, and the administrative safeguards established to shield your data against unauthorized exfiltration. By interacting with the SupperPlatter website, subscribing to our seasonal dining journals, or registering for private studio consultations at our 181 Mercer Street facility, you acknowledge the systematic data stewardship practices described herein. We recognize that trust forms the bedrock of every enduring dining relationship, and we remain steadfast in our duty to protect your personal privacy with total transparency. Should you possess any questions regarding this protocol, our dedicated legal and data governance team in New York stands ready to address your inquiries promptly.

2. Categories of Information Collected Across Digital Portals

During your interaction with our online atelier, we collect voluntary information that you actively submit through digital contact forms, consultation scheduling modules, and customer support channels. These voluntarily provided records typically encompass your full legal name, residential shipping address, electronic mail address, telephone contact number, and tailored culinary specifications such as dining table measurements and banquet seating capacities. If you submit inquiries concerning custom glazes or bespoke timber carving platters, we also archive your aesthetic notes and spatial constraints to facilitate precise architectural drafting by our ceramicists. We deliberately refrain from capturing sensitive biological, political, or philosophical markers, maintaining strict operational focus on data points strictly indispensable to the fulfillment of high-end dinnerware orders. Every submission is validated through automated sanitization filters to prevent the injection of erroneous or compromised metadata into our secure client repository.

In addition to voluntarily provided credentials, our hosting architecture automatically records standardized technical telemetry whenever your browser establishes an encrypted handshake with our web servers. This passive diagnostic telemetry includes your device Internet Protocol address, operating system configuration, browser engine version, referring uniform resource locator, localized timestamp, and sequential page visit chronology. These network telemetry packets are processed in aggregated, pseudo-anonymized formats strictly for systems health diagnostics, server load balancing, and malicious traffic mitigation. We employ this operational telemetry to detect potential cybersecurity intrusions, optimize asset delivery speeds across global content distribution networks, and verify that high-resolution ceramic galleries render smoothly on mobile and desktop viewports alike. Under no circumstances do we correlate raw server diagnostic packets with your personally identifiable customer account files without your prior express consent.

3. Lawful Grounds & Operational Objectives for Data Processing

We process your personal information strictly in accordance with recognized statutory legal frameworks, including contractual necessity, legitimate institutional interests, and unequivocal user consent under the California Consumer Privacy Act and the General Data Protection Regulation. Contractual necessity governs our handling of your shipping coordinates, telephone contact details, and electronic mail so that we may fabricate bespoke stoneware platters, generate courier manifests, and transmit timely delivery milestone notifications. Without access to these operational parameters, our logistics artisans would be unable to ensure that heavy, fragile ceramic crates arrive safely and promptly at your dining residence. Furthermore, our legal obligations under municipal and federal commercial tax codes necessitate the persistent archival of sales records and invoice metadata for a mandatory statutory period following purchase completion.

Our legitimate commercial interests encompass the continuous refinement of our culinary portfolio, the proactive prevention of payment fraud, and the maintenance of digital infrastructure stability. When we evaluate anonymous page dwell durations and navigational pathways through our technical monographs, we do so to discern which ceramic engineering topics resonate most profoundly with contemporary culinary hosts. If you have explicitly opted into receiving our seasonal supper monographs, we rely upon your affirmative consent to dispatch curated recipes, firing announcements, and table styling masterclasses. You retain the absolute statutory right to revoke your promotional marketing consent at any given moment by activating the automated unsubscribe link embedded in every dispatch or by transmitting a written directive directly to our compliance officers.

4. Data Minimization & Selective Third-Party Disclosures

SupperPlatter maintains a rigorous protocol of data minimization, guaranteeing that your personal credentials are never exposed beyond the immediate circle of vetted operational partners required to execute your dinnerware commissions. We collaborate solely with premium logistics freight carriers, encrypted payment processing gateways, enterprise cloud server hosts, and verified compliance auditors who have formally executed legally binding Data Processing Addenda. These specialized operational vendors are granted restricted access strictly to the precise data fields necessary to perform their specialized contractual duties, such as street delivery routing or credit card authorization. Our contractual covenants explicitly forbid all external service partners from retaining, copying, licensing, or re-identifying your personal credentials for any secondary or autonomous commercial objective. Routine audits are conducted by our internal security oversight committee to verify that vendor data handling practices conform precisely to our strict institutional standards.

Under exceptional statutory circumstances, SupperPlatter may be legally compelled to disclose specific client identifiers in response to valid judicial subpoenas, court warrants, or formal regulatory enforcement mandates issued by competent governmental authorities. Prior to releasing any archival documentation under such legal demands, our corporate counsel conducts a meticulous procedural review to substantiate the jurisdiction, validity, and scope of the request. We assertively contest overly broad or non-particularized information demands and provide affected patrons with immediate notification whenever legally permissible under applicable procedural law. Beyond these narrow statutory compulsions, we never share, distribute, or barter your customer files, guaranteeing that your dinner table investments remain entirely confidential between your household and our Mercer Street studio.

5. Cryptographic Architecture & Data Retention Governance

To ensure that your sensitive credentials remain perpetually impervious to illicit electronic interception, SupperPlatter enforces defense-in-depth cryptographic protocols across all physical and virtual server environments. All data transferred between your computing device and our online atelier is protected by modern Transport Layer Security protocol version 1.3 utilizing 256-bit Advanced Encryption Standard algorithms and authenticated key exchanges. Static database records containing client contact logs and transactional histories are stored in access-restricted, encrypted volumes safeguarded by multi-factor administrative authentication and automated intrusion detection sensors. Our technical development team undergoes regular third-party penetration testing and vulnerability assessments to identify and remediate potential attack surfaces before novel digital risks can manifest within production systems.

Personal identifiers are preserved within our active systems strictly for the minimal timeframe required to fulfill the specific operational objectives for which they were gathered. When you request a bespoke dining platter or reserve a Mercer Street tasting consultation, your project records remain active throughout the fabrication lifecycle and across the duration of our comprehensive ten-year stoneware structural warranty. Once all contractual obligations, warranty claim windows, and federal taxation retention mandates have expired, our data governance administrators execute cryptographic file wiping protocols that securely purge your identifiers from our primary storage arrays and redundant offsite disaster recovery backups. Should you request the accelerated erasure of your records prior to standard schedule expiration, our compliance team will fulfill your request subject only to mandatory statutory recordkeeping exemptions.

6. Your Statutory Rights & International Regulatory Protections

Regardless of your geographic jurisdiction, SupperPlatter affords comprehensive statutory rights regarding the control, verification, rectification, and disposal of your personal data records. You hold the unequivocal right to request formal confirmation as to whether our systems maintain any personal records associated with your identity, alongside an itemized ledger detailing processing purposes, recipient categories, and anticipated archival durations. If any element of your recorded information proves inaccurate or obsolete, you may demand prompt rectification or supplementary annotation to ensure your client record reflects current reality. Under statutory frameworks including the General Data Protection Regulation and the California Consumer Privacy Act, you may likewise request the permanent erasure of your personal identifiers or demand the restriction of specific automated processing activities.

To exercise any of your statutory privacy entitlements, you are warmly invited to submit a formal written inquiry to our Data Governance Concierge at our 181 Mercer Street facility or via electronic dispatch to care@supperplatter.com. We require reasonable identity verification prior to executing requests involving sensitive archival records to protect our patrons from fraudulent or unauthorized data alteration attempts. We never impose surcharges or discriminate against any patron in pricing, product availability, or service quality on the basis of exercising their lawful privacy protections. Our compliance team commits to processing and formally resolving all verified privacy requests within thirty calendar days of receipt, delivering full documentation of actions taken directly to your verified contact address.
