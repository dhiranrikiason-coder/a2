Thermal Physics & Metallurgy

The Science of Tabletop Temperature Preservation

A magnificent dinner is often ruined when hot entrees cool prematurely upon transfer to room-temperature serving dishes. Our stoneware composition solves this dilemma through deliberate material density.

By formulating our proprietary clay body with 32 percent calcined refractory feldspar, our platters achieve a thermal absorption coefficient of 1.48 W/m·K. When pre-warmed in a moderate 175°F warming oven for ten minutes, the platter serves as a gentle radiating heat reservoir that maintains food warmth throughout a prolonged multi-course supper.

From Kiln to Table

The Four Pillars of SupperPlatter Craftsmanship

Every piece undergoes a multi-week transformation from raw earth to an enduring culinary heirloom.

  • 1.
    Raw Mineral Sourcing: Sourced exclusively from pristine American clay deposits free of synthetic dyes and recycled urban aggregate.
  • 2.
    Dual Vitrification Firing: Bisque fired at 1,800°F followed by high-fire glaze maturation at cone 10 (2,340°F) for extreme vitreous density.
  • 3.
    Thermal Shock Stress Profiling: Every batch is tested from 400°F oven heat to room temperature counters to ensure total fracture resistance.
  • 4.
    Hand-Burnished Finishing: The base foot-ring is hand-polished with 400-grit diamond pads to prevent scratching delicate mahogany dining tables.

Table Guidance

Frequently Asked Hospitality Inquiries

Everything you need to know about thermal warming, dishwasher safety, and timber conditioning.

How should I pre-warm stoneware dinner platters prior to serving?
We recommend placing your ceramic stoneware platter in an oven set to 175°F to 200°F for eight to twelve minutes prior to plating. This charges the mineral thermal mass without overheating the rim, allowing the dish to be safely carried with standard linen napkins while preserving hot food temperatures at the dining table for upwards of 45 minutes.
Are SupperPlatter stoneware vessels safe for dishwasher and oven use?
Yes. All our ceramic stoneware pieces are fully vitrified at 2,340°F and are certified safe for conventional ovens up to 500°F, microwave reheating, and standard commercial dishwashers. To prevent thermal shock fracture, avoid moving the platter directly from a cold refrigerator into a scorching oven.
How do I clean and maintain the solid walnut carving boards?
End-grain walnut boards should always be hand-washed with lukewarm water and mild organic dish soap, then wiped immediately dry with a lint-free towel. Never submerge the timber in water or place it in a dishwasher. Re-apply organic food-grade mineral oil or our proprietary beeswax balm every four to six weeks to preserve natural grain suppleness.
Do you accommodate custom dimensions for bespoke dining tables?
Absolutely. Our Mercer Street atelier regularly collaborates with private homeowners and interior architects to create bespoke platter suites engineered to match specific dining table lengths, seating arrangements, and sideboard depths. Visit our Contact page to schedule a bespoke consultation.
